# Assets and typography

Veyli Colour is an original fictional beauty brand. No private client assets, real customer records, famous-brand imagery or claims are included.

Original packaging, shade and campaign imagery is documented by its media owner in [docs/IMAGE_PROVENANCE.md](docs/IMAGE_PROVENANCE.md) and [docs/image-prompts.json](docs/image-prompts.json). Closed opaque packages are shade-neutral; Satin Stroke's open shade views use their matching image. Colours on skin are illustrative editorial styling, not a prediction or virtual try-on.

Two intentional font families are locally bundled through exact Fontsource packages: Syne for display and Figtree for interface text. Only selected Latin WOFF2 weights are emitted. Complete SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices are in `docs/licenses/Syne-OFL.txt` and `docs/licenses/Figtree-OFL.txt`. No external font service is required.

The MIT code license does not replace font notices or generated-artwork provenance.
